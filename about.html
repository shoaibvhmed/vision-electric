Built in Chennai.

We are commercial electric vehicle manufacturers focused on practical loader vehicles, customer-led engineering and more affordable last-mile mobility.

We believe the future of commercial mobility should be electric, useful and accessible.

Our Story

Purpose before
everything.

Vision Innovative Products develops custom electric vehicles in Chennai for commercial use. Our focus is simple: build capable electric loaders that solve real operating needs, carry meaningful loads and reduce the cost of everyday deliveries.

Our loader vehicles are designed to carry about 550 kg, travel around 80–90 km per charge and help customers achieve deliveries at less than 20% of the cost of regular fuel-powered vehicles.
